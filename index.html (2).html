Weapons & Tier Catalog

Full GunModule stats (Damage, Ammo, Mag, Fire Gap, Spread, Recoil) organized by Tier.

GunModule & Gameplay Notes:
  • Automatic Fire: Kruger SR40 and Micro Drago are set to fire automatically at 1 shot per 0.05 s.
  • 38 Special: Uses .380 ACP ammo even though it's a .38 (possible config mismatch).
  • Headshots: The game features one-shot headshots, overriding standard body damage numbers.
  • Ruger LCP: Uses an older legacy script not included in this stats table.
  • Config Path: Edit ReplicatedStorage.Resource.Modules.GunModule to adjust weapon values.
